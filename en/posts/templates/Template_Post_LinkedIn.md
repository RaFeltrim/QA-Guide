## Filled Example

💡 **Have you ever confused severity with priority?**

A common mistake among beginner QAs is using severity and priority as synonyms. But they are different concepts that impact workflow in distinct ways.

✅ **Framework to differentiate:**

1. **Severity** = Technical impact of the bug
2. **Priority** = Business-defined order of correction
3. **High severity ≠ High priority**
4. **Low severity ≠ Low priority**
5. **Always validate with PO/Product Manager**

🔍 **Practical example:**
- A typo in the footer (low severity) on a checkout page causing sales loss (high priority)
- A login screen crash (high severity) for a feature launching next quarter (low priority)

📋 **Immediate checklist:**
- [ ] Classify technical impact (severity)
- [ ] Understand business impact (priority)
- [ ] Validate with PO before sending to development
- [ ] Document both fields in bug report
- [ ] Explain the difference to your team
