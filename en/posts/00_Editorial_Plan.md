# 00 - Editorial Plan

## Overview

This editorial plan defines the content strategy for QA-Guide, following the "CAIO" model that structures content creation in clear stages:

1. **Planning** - Definition of themes, calendar and goals
2. **Backlog** - List of ideas and topics to be addressed
3. **Execution** - Creation of posts and materials
4. **Tracking** - Performance monitoring and adjustments

## Objectives

- Create consistent and valuable content for the QA community
- Align posts with the technical content of QA-Guide
- Establish a regular publication frequency
- Facilitate discovery and practical application of concepts

## Target Audience

- QA Engineers at different levels (Intern → Senior)
- Developers interested in quality
- Product Owners and Stakeholders
- Students and beginners in the QA field

## Publication Channels

- LinkedIn (short and direct content)
- Personal blog/GitHub Pages (in-depth content)
- Instagram (educational carousels)
- Newsletter (monthly curation)

## Publication Frequency

- **Goal:** 2 posts per week
- **Distribution:** 1 technical post + 1 career/reflection post
- **Period:** 8 weeks (16 posts planned initially)

## Content Pillars

1. **QA Fundamentals and Mindset**
2. **Test Strategy** (pyramid, risk, criteria)
3. **API and Contract Testing**
4. **UI/E2E with Maintenance and Flakiness**
5. **Bug Triage and Communication** (severity vs priority)
6. **CI/CD and Gates**
7. **Performance** (k6/JMeter) and Observability
8. **Career** (intern→senior) and Portfolio

## Creation Process

1. Select theme from backlog
2. Identify related content in QA-Guide
3. Create draft using templates
4. Review technical consistency
5. Publish and monitor engagement
6. Update mapping with links

## Governance

- Every post must reference QA-Guide content
- Maintain style and formatting standards
- Update calendar after publication
- Record engagement metrics
